Manu Mātātahi

Manu Mātātahi is a youth-driven chorus on a mission to rejuvenate the art of barbershop singing amongst young people across the Waikato.

Founded in 2022, we welcome singers from a wide range of backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience. What connects us is a love of singing and the feeling of creating something special together. Through our shared passion for harmony, we've had opportunities to perform and travel throughout the country and overseas, including representing Aotearoa on the international stage in St Louis, USA in 2026\.

Inspired by kaupapa Māori and built on a strong sense of community, Manu Mātātahi blends elements of Te Ao Māori with the classic sound of barbershop harmony. The result is our own unique sound, weaving together the richness of Te Ao Māori with the ringing harmonies of the barbershop tradition.

We rehearse at 7pm on Thursdays in the Marian Catholic School Hall (access off Clyde Street), nau mai haere mai\! Come and join us some time\!

Co-directors: Harley Pitihira and Nathan Harper
Chorus Manager: Tylar Te Uira
