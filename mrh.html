Mighty River Harmony

Mighty River Harmony is a chorus of men from the greater Waikato. We sing in four-part harmony in the barbershop style, aiming to entertain a wide audience with engaging songs performed to high standards. We seek to perfect our craft in an atmosphere of fun and camaraderie, to be innovative, and to attract men of all ages who have the potential and drive to be good singers and performers. We provide a learning environment that encourages continuous improvement for both individual and group satisfaction. If you would like to find out more you are most welcome to attend any of our rehearsals (for details see below).

We give several public concerts each year, where you can hear and see us perform. You can also hire the chorus or a quartet to sing for that special occasion – enquire here.

Our Vision

"Men in Harmony for Everyone in Harmony."

Our Mission

"Mighty River Harmony brings men together to enrich lives through singing harmony."

What we do at rehearsals
We use our rehearsal time to learn new songs and improve the performance standard of the songs already in our repertoire. We spend a lot of time trying to perfect our "craft" – all of the basic qualities of good barbershop singing.
At certain times of the year we concentrate on our "contest package" (the two songs that we will compete with at the annual competition), songs for upcoming public performances or songs for the season, such as Christmas. At rehearsals we have loads of fun while helping each other to improve our overall performance to a level that will leave the audience exhilarated!

Visit a rehearsal
We practice every Tuesday evening from 7.30pm to about 9.30pm. We meet at the Marian School Hall, Clyde Street (entrance near the corner of Firth and Clyde Streets), Hamilton East.

What is Barbershop Harmony?
Barbershop harmony is an *a cappella* (unaccompanied) style of singing with four voices. The ‘lead’ usually sings the melody, with the ‘tenor’ harmonizing above the lead. The ‘bass’ sings the lowest harmonizing notes and the ‘baritone’ provides the in-between notes, either above or below the lead to make chords (specifically, dominant-type or ‘barbershop’ sevenths) that give barbershop its distinctive, full sound.

We are a chorus rather than a choir, because of our strong emphasis on performance. Performing barbershop music incorporates movement, expression and emotion in order to entertain. Barbershop singing originated in the USA in the 19th century, in barbers’ shops and on street corners, in the tradition of recreational music. Today Barbershop singing is flourishing world-wide.

The Barbershop Harmony Society has approximately 14,000 active members in the United States and Canada, with more than 70,000 total singers participating worldwide across various affiliated international organizations, as reported in the BHS fact sheet.

Mighty River Harmony is affiliated to BHS via BHNZ – Barbershop Harmony New Zealand. There are currently over 700 members in New Zealand in 25 choruses and 40 quartets.

The Chorus started in 1992 as the Hamilton Hair Razors Barbershop Chorus, with Jill Rodgers as the Musical Director. Lloyd Smith, a former member of gold medal-winning chorus in US, The Vocal Majority, was instrumental in providing expertise and guidance in the style. Auckland’s City of Sails Chorus provided help and encouragement. The Chorus changed its name in 2003 to Mighty River Harmony.

Barbershop singing has some elements of a competitive sport, and in 1996 we were the champion New Zealand chorus. Attending the 1998 Pan Pacific Convention in Hawaii with 51 members was a major highlight for the Chorus. We hosted the 1995, 2003, 2010 and 2015 NZ Conventions of Barbershop Singers at Founders Theatre. In 2017 we won the New Zealand Gold Medal again at the Pan Pacific competition in Sydney. In 2024 and 2025 we were the top male chorus.
