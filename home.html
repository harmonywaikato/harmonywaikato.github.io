Welcome to Harmony Waikato

Harmony Waikato is a vibrant community of singers united by a shared love of barbershop singing. Through our family of choruses and quartets, we provide opportunities for people across the Waikato to sing barbershop music at every level.

From first-time singers to experienced performers, we offer opportunities to learn, perform, compete, and build lifelong friendships through the art of barbershop.

Quartets

The Usual Suspects

The Usual Suspects

Tenor: Keegan Davies

Lead: Nathan Harper

Baritone: Taks Southon

Bass: Harley Pitihira

The Usual Suspects are Barbershop Harmony New Zealand's 2023 Champions, hailing from Kirikiriroa Hamilton. Formed in 2020, the quartet's mission is to share their passion for barbershop a-cappella singing with audiences in New Zealand, and ultimately the world.

Harley, Takiri, Keegan and Nathan are highly entertaining performers, with an energetic style and repertoire that appeals to audiences of all ages and backgrounds. Contest audiences voted them the 'most entertaining' quartet back-to-back in 2022 and 2023, and they placed 25th in the world in 2024 in Cleveland.

Four Square

Four Square

Tenor: Nio Vuna

Lead: Caleb Bayley-Conder

Baritone: Hunter Geary

Bass: Kiri Nabuti

Four Square are a Waikato-based men's quartet founded in February of 2025! These four brothers have gone on to compete in two national conventions so far, making the finals in both years and earning the Most Improved award at the 2026 national convention.

The boys' goal is to get more young people invested into barbershop like they have, hoping that their performances resonate with those who watch their performances. Four Square are often remembered by audiences for their stage presence and natural charisma with one another.

REDemption

REDemption

Tenor: Kiri Crossland

Lead: Maree Powell

Baritone: Lynn Jamieson

Bass: Rebecca Clewlow

Originally known as RED Quartet, Rebecca, Lynn, and Maree have been harmonising together for over 16 years. Their journey has come full circle with the addition of their newest member Kiri who was introduced to barbershop by the trio as a child. As RED, the quartet earned recognition in the Sweet Adelines New Zealand Quartet Competition, earning a fourth place medal in the 2017 national contest. Re-forming as REDemption, they went on to place tenth in their first BHNZ competition. These four ladies love a laugh, and quartet gives them the perfect excuse to meet up every week.

Belladonna

Belladonna

Tenor: Rachel

Lead: Natassja

Baritone: Rebecca

Bass: Laura

Belladonna is our freshest upper voice quartet, bringing together Rachel, Rebecca, Laura and Natassja, who all share a strong barbershop background through the Young Singers in Harmony programme. Formed in late 2025, their quartet grew from four friends who simply loved singing together and the sound they created. Following a successful Northern Regional contest, they made an impressive national debut, winning the Novice category and placing 9th overall in the Open Quartet contest.
