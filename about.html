About Harmony Waikato

Harmony Waikato is a vibrant community of singers united by a shared love of barbershop singing. Through our family of choruses and quartets, we provide opportunities for people across the Waikato to sing barbershop music at every level.

From first-time singers to experienced performers, we offer opportunities to learn, perform, compete, and build lifelong friendships through the art of barbershop.

Harmony Waikato was born out of Mighty River Harmony, a men’s barbershop chorus, first known as the Hamilton Hair Razors, established in the 1990s. In 2021, the men of Mighty River Harmony recognised a need for a place for young people to sing barbershop harmony after leaving high school and youth chorus Manu Mātātahi was born.

A year later, the two choruses formalised their structure into Harmony Waikato, the Waikato Chapter of Barbershop Harmony New Zealand. Since then we have added Vox Collective Kirikiriroa to the whānau, creating a space for people of all ages and abilities to sing barbershop.

We are registered as a charitable entity. See our registration as the "Waikato Chapter of the New Zealand Association of Barbershop Singers Incorporated" at the Charities Register.
